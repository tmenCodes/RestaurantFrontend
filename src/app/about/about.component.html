About Us

Yummy is the first solo project of Chef Walter White. Inspired by Walter's upbringing in downtown Minneapolis and his experience leading the city's most celebrated kitchens, Yummy is a neighborhood seasonal restaurant elevated by fine dining training and technique.

Located on Adam Street, MN, the dining room and bar occupy a series of rooms with floor-to-16-foot-ceiling windows leading to an open kitchen. The food at Yummy is rooted in Asian cuisine but draws inspiration from around the world — primarily the flavors from our childhoods, traveling, and the diverse culinary scene of Minneapolis.
